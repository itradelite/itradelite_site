About

iTradeLite is a personal tool: one engineer’s rule-based system for picking ASX trades for his own money. There are no customers and nothing is for sale.

Why it exists

To make trading decisions explicit and repeatable: if a trade plan can’t be written down as rules and checked afterwards, it isn’t a system. Every pick comes with an entry, a stop, an exit plan and a size, and every pick is later scored in the track record — including when the answer is “an index ETF would have done better”.

What it is not

  • Not financial advice, and not personalised to anyone else’s situation.
  • Not proven: the Strategy decay audit currently finds no edge distinguishable from luck in the strategies with enough history.
  • Not real-time: prices are end-of-day for the night scan and delayed 15-minute bars intraday.

How it runs

A scheduled pipeline (GitHub Actions) runs the scans on ASX trading days and publishes JSON files; this site is a static page that renders them. See How it works for the rules and schedule.
