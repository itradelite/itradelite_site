The Philosophy

Most signals are vague. iTradeLite is intentionally explicit. We believe that if a trade plan can't be explained and repeated by a machine, it isn't a system—it's just vibes.

  • Quantitative Logic: Every setup is based on mathematical price action, not intuition.
  • Risk First: We prioritize the "Stop" and "Position Size" over the potential profit.
  • Regime Gating: We toggle strategies off when the broader market environment is unfavorable.

What it is NOT

iTradeLite is a rule-based data output generated from end-of-day market statistics. It is not a financial advisory service.
