About iTradeLite

A small site that does one job: publish a daily rule-based list with clear entries, stops and modelled risk.

View Today’s List

What iTradeLite is

iTradeLite is a rule-based scanner that produces a daily list of setups from end-of-day market data. The goal is clarity and repeatability: the same rules produce the same outputs.

  • Simple daily workflow
  • Explicit entry + stop
  • Modelled position sizing (A$ risk)
  • Market regime gating (risk-on / risk-off)

What iTradeLite is not

  • Not financial advice
  • Not personalised to your portfolio, taxes, or risk profile
  • Not a promise of profitability
  • Not a high-frequency/intraday execution system
Education only: This site is for informational and educational purposes. Markets involve risk. You are responsible for your own decisions.

Why it exists

Most “signals” online are vague. iTradeLite is intentionally explicit: show the setup, show the level, show the stop, show the modelled risk.

If it can’t be explained and repeated, it’s not a system — it’s vibes.
