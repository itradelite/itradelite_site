Rule-based ASX picks.
Checked, then audited.

A personal tool. Each trading night it scans ASX daily bars and recommends at most two orders for the next session, with entry, stop, exit plan and position size. During the day it re-checks those picks against live prices. Every published pick is replayed in a track record against simply buying an ASX200 ETF.

 EVERY PICK, REPLAYED

Track record

Realistic fills and costs, compared with an ASX200 ETF, plus a comparison of exit policies (fixed days vs trailing stops).

 IS IT LUCK?

Strategy decay

Each strategy tested in isolation against a random-entry baseline. Several currently show no edge distinguishable from noise, and the page says so.
