The Methodology

A transparent, rule-based daily list of setups. Built to be useful first, improved later.

The "Today" Dashboard

Each row is a setup candidate produced by fixed rules using end-of-day (daily) market data.

Status Classification:

  • Valid: Passes all required gates.
  • Watch: Interesting but missing confirmation (e.g., volume, price distance).

Risk & Position Sizing

Position size is derived mathematically from the distance between your Entry and Stop.

Size = (Account Equity × Risk%) / (Entry - Stop)

Modelled using A$100,000 portfolio and 1% risk per trade.

Active Strategies

Strategy 01

Trend Pullback

Looks for an uptrend plus a pullback toward a moving average area, aiming for better risk/reward than chasing highs.

Strategy 02

Momentum Breakout

Looks for a breakout above prior highs with confirming strength (RSI, volume). Typically uses STOP-LIMIT orders.

Strategy 03

Mean Reversion

Identifies oversold conditions inside long-term uptrends, targeting a bounce back toward the mean while avoiding "falling knives".

Market Regime (Risk-On / Risk-Off)

Setups are gated by the broader market environment. If the regime is "risk-off", the system may suppress trend and momentum style entries for safety.
