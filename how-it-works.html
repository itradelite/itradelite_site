The Methodology

Our process moves from raw end-of-day market data to a disciplined, rule-based trade plan using institutional risk management.

01. Risk Sizing Model

Position sizing is the most critical component of the system. We do not use arbitrary share counts. Instead, size is derived mathematically based on the "Risk-to-Stop" distance.

Institutional Calculation
Shares = (Portfolio Equity × Risk %) ÷ (Entry - Stop)

Example: On a $100k portfolio risking 1% ($1,000), if the distance between your entry and stop is $0.50, the system mandates a size of 2,000 shares.

02. Setup Archetypes

TYPE_01

Trend Pullback

Identifying institutional "buy zones" within established uptrends. We look for price to mean-revert to key moving averages before resuming the primary trend.

TYPE_02

Momentum Breakout

Scanning for high-volume expansions above defined resistance levels. These setups utilize Stop-Limit orders to ensure we only enter on confirmed strength.

TYPE_03

Mean Reversion

Spotting extreme oversold conditions within long-term bullish structures. This targets "rubber band" effects where price has stretched too far from its value area.

03. Market Regime Gating

Strategy execution is binary based on the Market Regime. We utilize a proprietary filter (Risk-On / Risk-Off) to determine participation:

  • Risk-On: All strategies active. Full exposure allowed.
  • Risk-Off: Momentum and Breakout setups are gated. Only high-conviction pullbacks or cash positions are recommended.
