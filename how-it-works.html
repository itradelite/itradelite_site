How iTradeLite Works

A transparent, rule-based daily list of setups. Built to be useful first, improved later.

What you’re looking at on the “Today” page

Each row is a setup candidate produced by fixed rules using end-of-day (daily) market data. The list is the same for everyone.

Entry Stop Risk (A$)

Risk (A$) is modelled using a A$100,000 portfolio and 1% risk per trade (position size derived from entry–stop distance).

“Valid” vs “Watch”

  • Valid: the setup passes all required gates.
  • Watch: interesting but missing one or more gates (e.g. not close enough to trigger, not enough volume confirmation, etc.).

Data model & constraints (important)

  • Daily bars (OHLCV) with typical Yahoo Finance limitations.
  • No intraday confirmation inside this page (yet).
  • Signals are educational — execution, liquidity, spreads, and slippage are real.
Practical note: if Yahoo’s daily data is late (common), the site can show “stale” conditions and should not be treated as a same-day instruction set.

Setup types (current)

1) Trend Pullback

Looks for an uptrend plus a pullback toward a moving average area, aiming to enter with better risk/reward than chasing highs.

2) Momentum Breakout

Looks for a breakout above prior highs with confirming strength conditions (e.g. RSI zone, volume). Entry is typically via STOP-LIMIT.

3) Mean Reversion

Looks for oversold conditions inside a long-term uptrend (avoid “falling knives”) and targets a bounce back toward the mean.

Risk sizing (why positions differ)

Position size is calculated from:

  • Account equity (modelled)
  • Risk per trade (e.g. 1%)
  • Entry → stop distance

Smaller entry–stop distance ⇒ larger size. Larger distance ⇒ smaller size.

Market regime (Risk-On / Risk-Off)

Some setups can be gated by the market regime. If the regime is “risk-off”, the system can choose to avoid trend/momentum style entries.
