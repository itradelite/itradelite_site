The Methodology

How we move from raw end-of-day data to a disciplined trade plan.

Risk Model

Position size is derived mathematically based on the distance between your entry and your stop-loss.

Position Size = (Equity × Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Price)

We model this using a standard A$100,000 portfolio and a 1% risk-per-trade constraint.
