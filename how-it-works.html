The Methodology.

Raw market data processed through institutional risk frameworks to produce a disciplined, rule-based execution plan.

01. Risk Sizing Model

Position sizing is the engine of the system. We do not use arbitrary share counts. Every trade is sized mathematically based on the "Risk-to-Stop" distance.

Institutional Calculation
Shares = (Equity × Risk %) ÷ (Entry - Stop)

Example: On a $100k portfolio risking 1% ($1,000), if the distance between your entry and stop is $0.50, the system mandates a size of 2,000 shares.

02. Setup Archetypes

TYPE_01

Trend Pullback

Identifying institutional "buy zones" within uptrends. We target mean-reversion to key moving averages before trend resumption.

TYPE_02

Momentum Breakout

Scanning for high-volume expansions above resistance. These utilize Stop-Limit orders to ensure entry on confirmed strength.

TYPE_03

Mean Reversion

Spotting extreme oversold conditions in bullish structures. Targeting "rubber band" effects when price overextends from value.

03. Market Regime Gating

Strategy execution is binary. We utilize a proprietary filter (Risk-On / Risk-Off) to determine capital participation:

  • ● Risk-On: All strategies active. Full exposure allowed.
  • ● Risk-Off: Momentum setups gated. Only high-conviction pullbacks allowed.
