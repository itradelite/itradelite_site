How to read this page

LIMIT buys only at or below the limit. STOP buys once price trades up through the trigger. STOP-LIMIT triggers like a stop but pays no more than the limit. Entry ref is the worst-case fill used for sizing.

Qty = min(risk budget ÷ |entry ref − stop|, portfolio × max position % ÷ entry ref, portfolio ÷ entry ref). (cap) means a cap bound; hover it for the maths. Risk is the actual A$ lost if the initial stop is hit.

Sentiment chips: failed and no data components are excluded from the score, never counted as neutral.
